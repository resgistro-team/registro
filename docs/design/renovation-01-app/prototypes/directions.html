All three directions borrow Luma's best move, a cover on every event, and make the covers ours: each one is a real piece of the night map around campus.

Cover first

Every event leads with its light cover, then the title over its foot. The page has a place, not a heading on black.

Facts you can act on

Add to calendar and Open map sit next to the date and place, the two things people do after deciding.

Honest turnout

A count and a fill bar. No fake avatars: the API rightly keeps attendee names private, so we don't invent faces.

Ember means scarce

The only warm color on the page marks spots running out, so it always means act now.

Light covers

Each event gets a window onto the real night map near campus, picked from its ID so it never changes, and tinted by category. No uploads, no extra downloads, and no two neighbours look alike. Hosts can still add their own photo later.

Recommendation: A

It fixes the complaint directly. Events are on the first screen, the page is light on this 8 GB laptop, and the dive survives as a one-time intro that becomes the band. B keeps the spectacle but leaves browsing below the fold. C is the boldest look but trades away how much fits on a screen.

Covers, day groups and the new event page come with any choice.

# Sign-in background credits

The clips in `clips/` are cut from nine Mixkit stock clips, used under the Mixkit Stock Video Free License (commercial use allowed, no attribution required). Each was trimmed, colour-matched and compressed for the web. The sign-in page plays them in a new random order on every visit, crossfading between them. It covers the range of what's on: parties, coding, conferences, seminars and festivals, with two drone shots.
